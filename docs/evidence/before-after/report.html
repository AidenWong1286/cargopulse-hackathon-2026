Method

Every flight and disruption is planned by both methods (a paired design), and all inference is per flight or per disruption, never per AWB, because AWBs on one flight are not independent. Protect's overbooking was calibrated on 200 separate flights, using the simple rule only, to the 11.28% reference offload rate; the test flights and disruptions use fresh seeds that were not used during development. Confidence intervals are 95% paired percentile bootstraps (10,000 resamples) with ratio statistics recomputed in every resample. Significance uses the Wilcoxon signed-rank test (normal approximation, tie and continuity corrected) and an exact sign test; secondary endpoints are Holm-adjusted. The below-5% claim requires the one-sided 95% upper bound to be below 5%.

Limits. All data is synthetic, from seeded generators. Protect checks total weight and volume only, not hold positions, balance or dangerous-goods separation, and the model has no booking no-shows, so the trimmed-overbooking result illustrates the method rather than a booking policy. Recover counts proposed compliant assignments, not actual deliveries. These results show the method works under stated assumptions; they are not Cathay operational results.
